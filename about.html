How Skynet works

A study partner that shows its sources.

Skynet is a retrieval chatbot built on Chatling for students in ITOM 6216, Generative AI for Business. It answers from six course files and names the file it used, so you can check every answer against the slides.

Back to home

The problem

Course info is scattered.

Before Skynet, answering "what's due and how does it connect to the lecture?" meant opening the syllabus, the course guide and up to five slide decks. That costs time right when you need the answer fastest: the night before a lab is due.

  • Due dates sit in the syllabus and Canvas.
  • Concepts like RAG, prompt engineering and agents are spread across five slide decks.
  • Final project rules (5-minute limit, 3 PDF slides, live demo on the classroom computer) are buried in the course guide.
  • Skynet puts all of it behind one chat box.

Lecture 3 in action

What happens when you hit send

Skynet uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the same pipeline taught in Class 3. The language model never answers from memory alone.

You ask

Type a question such as "What's the rubric for the final report?"

Retrieve

Skynet searches its knowledge base of six course files for the passages that match your question.

Augment

Those passages are handed to the language model as context, along with the instruction to answer only from course files.

Answer

You get a plain-language reply that names the source file, for example Course_Guide.txt or Class4_Slides.txt.

The conversation

How a session runs

Welcome→Share name, email, job role→Ask anything→Sourced answer↻

After the welcome message, Skynet asks for your first and last name, email and preferred job role so it can greet you by name. Then you can ask as many questions as you like in a loop.

Guardrails

It won't guess.

If the answer isn't in the course files, such as Class 6 material or something said only in class, Skynet says so and points you to the professor's email. Its instructions are to answer only from the course files and to name the files it uses.

Your data

What gets stored, and where

Inside the Skynet chat (Chatling)

  • Your name, email and preferred job role are saved as a contact.
  • Your questions are visible to the bot's owner in the Chatling dashboard.
  • Don't type grades, passwords or anything private.

On this website

  • Flashcard progress is saved in your own browser (localStorage).
  • This site has no login, no tracking scripts and no database.
  • Clearing your browser data or pressing "Reset progress" wipes it.

FAQ

Is Skynet an official SMU tool?

No. Skynet Study is a mock business created by a student for ITOM 6216. Check Canvas and the syllabus for anything grade-critical.

Why does it ask for my email?

The Chatling flow saves each student as a contact so the bot can personalize replies. You can type a nickname if you prefer.

Will it cover Class 6?

Not yet. The knowledge base covers Lectures 1 to 5 and the course guide. New slides get added after each class.

Can it do my lab for me?

It explains concepts and logistics. The course AI policy still applies: you are responsible for what you submit, and some reflections must be your own work.
